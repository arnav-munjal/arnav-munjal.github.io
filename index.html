Cocoa Butter Press Assembly

Three-person SolidWorks design of a compact, low-cost press. I designed the collection sub-assembly.
Team
3 members
My module
Collection sub-assembly
CAD
SolidWorks
Date
Jan 2026 – May 2026

The problem

Build a compact, low-cost press that turns cocoa beans into cocoa butter, is simple to assemble, and lets the user pull the butter out without taking the machine apart. The team split it into three modules: frame, pressing system, and collection system.

What I did

  • Designed the 12-item collection sub-assembly in SolidWorks (perforated canister, piston, hydraulic ram, reservoir with sliding cap, support plate, and slide-out collection tray), taking each part from dimensioned hand sketch to finished 3D model.
  • Combined the collection, frame, and hydraulic press sub-assemblies into one fully defined top-level assembly, resolving mismatched dimensions and mates with teammates, then built a mate-driven motion animation of the complete press cycle.
  • Documented the collection sub-assembly for fabrication with dimensioned working drawings, a BOM-linked exploded view, and a 7-step assembly instruction manual, and presented the design in the team's final report, slide deck, and video report.

What I learned

The hardest part was integration. Mismatched dimensions between modules broke mates in the final assembly, and fixing them showed how much shared reference dimensions and early communication matter on a team build.

Bearing Fault Diagnosis for Condition Monitoring

Vibration analysis and failure prediction for drive-end motor bearings, using the Case Western Reserve University dataset
Methods
FFT, regression, RK4
Data
12 kHz, 2 HP motor
Tools
MATLAB, signal processing
Date
Jan 2026 – May 2026

The problem

Given accelerometer data from a motor's drive-end bearing, tell a healthy bearing from a damaged one, estimate how bad the damage is, and predict when it will fail.

What I did

  • Analyzed CWRU bearing vibration data in MATLAB with Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) analysis, showing faulted bearings spread energy across 0–5 kHz at up to 1.0 g, versus below 2 kHz and 0.2 g when healthy.
  • Built 4 regression models relating fault diameter to RMS acceleration, selecting the cubic fit (R² = 0.96) and rejecting the 4th-order fit as overfit.
  • Reconstructed a missing peak with Newton and spline interpolation and compared relative error across methods.
  • Solved for motor speed with Newton-Raphson and secant methods, landing within 0.04% of the documented 1797 RPM.
  • Predicted bearing failure at 225 hours with a Runge-Kutta (RK4) wear model to support predictive maintenance, and showed why Euler's truncation error overestimates remaining life.

Cobra Pocket Watch

Solo concept-to-prototype design: a pocket watch whose chain is replaced by wrapping cobras, drawn from Hindu culture
Build
FDM 3D print
Role
Solo project
CAD
SolidWorks
Date
Jan 2026 – May 2026

The idea

A pocket watch goes everywhere, so it is a natural way to carry a piece of your culture with you. Cobras are central in Hindu culture, and here they replace the chain and wrap around the watch body. The concept came from the "unusual connection" ideation method and the "cover or wrap" and "apply an existing mechanism in a new way" design heuristics.

What I did

  • Carried the design from ideation sketches through multi-view, section, and assembly hand drawings to a full SolidWorks assembly, using revolve, shell, extrude, and sweep-and-loft features.
  • Specified GD&T controls for each part and designed three interference fits (ball-and-socket snake joints, hinged cover) with 0.04 ± 0.005 in and 0.05 ± 0.005 in mating dimensions, a 0.010 in nominal interference.
  • Produced part working drawings and a BOM-linked exploded view, then 3D printed and assembled a working prototype that validated fit and manufacturability.
